# Need cards: run sheet for the facilitator

One page for whoever runs the room. You need one link, the **facilitator link**, which Sean sends you. It opens the board. No sign-in. Anyone who has that link can run the room, so keep it to facilitators.

Nothing is timed. You move the room on when it's ready.

## Before people arrive

1. Open the facilitator link on a laptop. This is the board.
2. If some people will type the address instead of scanning: on **Settings & links**, copy the table link, make a short link from it at a link shortener (tinyurl.com lets you pick the name), and paste it into **Short link to type**. It then prints on every table card and shows on the wall.
3. Click **Table cards** (top right) and print one per table. Each has a QR code that opens that table's form.
4. Click **Open the wall** and put it on the projector. It shows counts only, never anyone's words.

## 1. Need cards

The bar at the top of the board says where the room is. It starts on **Need cards**.

At each table, one person scans the table's QR code. They see one page:

- **Your table's flip statement**: "Right now…, but we want…". Written once.
- **Add a need card**: the need, then G, G+ or B. "Written by" (student, adult, our table) is optional.

Each card shows up on the board as it is sent, word for word. A table can fix or remove its own cards.

On paper instead? On the board, **Cards & groups → Add need cards from paper**: type them in, or photograph the cards and check what AI read before confirming.

## 2. Grouping

Click **Grouping** on the bar. Tables see a thank-you; they can still add a late card.

On **Cards & groups**:

1. **Suggest groups** asks AI for a first draft. Each group shows how many tables raised it and its G / G+ / B counts. A **?** means tables tagged those cards differently.
2. Make it yours: rename groups, move cards, reorder, change a group's column, add or remove groups. Cards no group claims wait in **Doesn't fit yet**.
3. **Publish for voting** freezes the groups.
4. **Make posters** builds one slide per G and G+ group, with room for dots, plus parking-lot pages for the B needs. Print them and put them up.

Wait a few seconds after your last change before you publish.

## 3. Gallery walk

Click **Gallery walk**. Devices and the wall tell people to put their dots on the posters.

When the dots are down, on **Vote & results** type each poster's dot count: students, adults, and "?" for dots you can't tell apart. Or use **Add vote dots from paper** with a photo of each poster, and check the numbers against the paper before confirming.

## 4. Read-out

Click **Read-out**. The wall and devices show the G and G+ priorities ranked by dots.

## Afterwards

**Record & results → Make the results document** writes a Google Doc from the record: the ranked priorities, every card as written, and the parking lot. AI writes none of it.

## If something goes wrong

- A device shows the wrong step: click the step again on the board.
- A table can't get on: read them the table link (**Copy table link**, top right) or enter their cards from paper.
- The AI draft fails or looks wrong: group by hand. Everything else works without AI.
- The whole thing is down: the Google Form fallback has the same four fields.

More steps exist (signals, flip-statement dots, voting on devices, a guidance test drive). **Settings & links → Steps → Switch to full agenda** turns them on. You don't need them for this.
